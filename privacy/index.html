This is a local foundation preview, not a live intimate-data service.

What this preview does

It shows the visual direction, mode explanations, and three non-explicit sample ideas. It has no registration, payments, preference saving, analytics, advertising trackers, or third-party media calls. The preview acknowledgement stays in memory and resets when you reload.

The approach we are building toward

Each partner will have an independent account. Private answers will not be shown to partners. Shared suggestions will require two independent YES responses and explicit permission to share matches. Maybe will mean discussion, never automatic eligibility.

A revealed match necessarily reveals a shared interest. No matching system can promise to eliminate every inference. You will be able to revoke sharing, dissolve a relationship, and delete intimate records. These capabilities are designed but are not implemented in this preview.

Clear limits, honest promises

We do not claim end-to-end encryption. The planned service will process private answers to calculate approved matches, with application encryption and restricted operational access. Deployment and authentication providers may process network or account information. Their policies and retention must be finalized before a public launch.

Adults only

CheapSeggs is intended only for consenting adults aged 18 and older. The preview acknowledgement is not age verification. Real adult-content access will depend on legal review and the age-assurance requirements of supported locations.

Before launch

A full privacy notice, terms, support contact, approved market list, retention schedule, and deletion process must be published and tested before accounts open. No data has been uploaded to a live service as part of this foundation.
