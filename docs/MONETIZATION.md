Rank the experiment: (1) free 25-question invitation-based core, including usable summaries, all eight dimensions when coverage/permission allow, skips, dealbreakers and privacy controls; (2) optional one-time 50/100-depth editorial assessment, testing **$4.99 versus $9.99 per two-person assessment**, after a successful free pilot and written processor suitability; (3) future gift/occasion bundles only after refund/recipient semantics work. No subscription initially: pre-date assessment demand is episodic and encouraging repeated probing is a poor retention model. Never sell the other person's answers, private dealbreaker identities, a positive verdict, release permission or an intimate-topic opt-in. The free core cannot withhold a discovered hard-conflict warning behind payment; a scope-approved warning is free, while an unauthorized private rule remains excluded from shared reporting entirely.

One sponsor pays for one assessment credit covering both participants **without couple membership**. Reserve on joint selection of a paid deck, consume only on the first mutually released paid report. Consent and access expiry stay independent of purchase. An invitee need not pay or accept intimate topics because someone bought a credit. Proposed pilot rule: if the counterpart declines, withdraws or times out before first paid release, unreserve/return the credit; offer a full refund of an unused credit within 14 days, subject to any stronger applicable rights. After release, refund/support policy and statutory rights remain clear; a chargeback/refund revokes that source's paid access but never restores deleted answers or overrides disclosure. No paid extension of the counterpart's authorization and no refund-status disclosure to them. Scope-check report reads independently of ownership. Current `canAccessPack` does not implement service credits; [Data model](DATA_MODEL.md#planned-chemistry-check-entities-no-migration-yet) plans the necessary fulfillment distinction.

The anti-probing budget has a commercial cost: **do not assume an immediate free 25-question report → paid 100-question report for the same pair**. Offer an optional deeper deck chosen by both before their first result release, or a later assessment after the 30-day pair/family release cooldown. Show sample report structure and exact scope/price first; the free assessment remains usable with no withheld verdict or surprise charge. An interest survey after a free report is uncharged research, not an available instant upgrade. Disable checkout for a same-pair result that cannot be released within its advertised window. Paid upgrades, new question-bank versions and expansion names cannot evade the stable bank-family budget. This friction may favor Chemistry Check as a free acquisition funnel over an immediate revenue product; validate it before setting revenue targets.

Assess acquisition independently from payment: `chemistry_entry`, `chemistry_started`, `chemistry_invite_created`, `chemistry_invite_accepted`, `chemistry_submitted`, `chemistry_report_released`, `chemistry_checkout_started`, `chemistry_purchase_fulfilled` are aggregate event enums only. Keep invite reasons, identities/pair IDs, responses, score bands, dimension coverage, sexual opt-ins, skipped counts, dealbreakers and outcome-derived prompts out of analytics. Report independent submissions per accepted participant and released assessments per accepted invitation, not sexual compatibility or “good matches.” Own progress/release approval events must not become counterpart-visible notifications or marketing targeting. No sales email based on an unfavorable result. Measurement uses the same consented aggregate/cohort rules as the existing taxonomy.

Validation hypotheses: 15–20 diverse adult pairs in moderated non-sensitive tests; ≥90% understand assessment acceptance versus result release versus couple consent; 200 accepted assessment invitations in an opt-in pilot; target ≥50% reach a mutually released report within 7 days without reminders that disclose counterpart progress. A premium-interest test comes after a free report, not a surprise paywall after intimate disclosure. Seek 30 paid reports and refund/support evidence before broader paid rollout; sample sizes are operational discovery, not proof of demand or predictive validity. Paid conversion denominator is eligible **accepted assessments**, with abandoned/expired invitations separately visible in aggregates. Do not conflate individual MAU, pairs, credits sold and released reports.

Under the existing **hypothetical** high-risk payment/refund/dispute assumptions, $4.99/$9.99 give about $3.64/$7.72 contribution before support, content, services, underwriting and age checks. Charging for a second participant would damage the acquisition loop. Actual reserve/minimum fees and two-account support can make small sales uneconomic. Paid Chemistry Check is a separate conditional experiment after the core pack pilot, or a replacement only if its own funnel data is materially better; no confident new revenue forecast is justified now.

### Wheel and recipe free versus paid

Free permanently includes an actually useful wheel, basic recipe generation, core catalog discovery, essential private matching, all exclusions, revocation, privacy/deletion controls, accessibility and safety information. Paid content can add themed wheels, curated recipe collections, larger packs, seasonal artwork and advanced editorial personalization. Saving a few favorites should be free; large collections and custom wheel tooling may become paid after users show value. Boundaries, membership security and deletion are never paywalled.

No upsell during a spin/result discussion or recipe progression. Show packs in the menu and at a natural end of a session. A locked topic may show a safe public description, but must not reveal that a partner selected it. Never display “your partner wants this premium activity.” Treat declined checkout as a normal navigation event.

## Ownership and separation rule

**One purchaser owns the purchased library; both active partners can use it together while linked.** Entitlements belong to an individual account or gift recipient, not the relationship. The relationship borrows access; it does not become another owner. One person never pays twice just to include their partner.

- One-time ownership lasts for the purchased content version/library entitlement under disclosed service terms; no promise of every future release or perpetual hosting.
- On separation, the purchaser keeps their library. The former partner loses borrowed access; their own purchases stay. All mutual preferences/shared results stay invalid regardless of ownership.
- In a new relationship, purchaser content can be shared again, but old preferences/matches never move. Shared usage checks the selected relationship only, with an abuse review of mass relationship cycling before expansion.
- Gift claims grant ownership to the recipient. The purchaser receives fulfillment status, not identity, intimate use or preferences. For the initial pilot, defer gifting to avoid complex lifecycle rules.
- Full refund/chargeback revokes or suspends grants from that purchase, including borrowed access. Other independently valid grants still work. Partial refunds require explicit line-item allocation; pilot supports only full-bundle refunds.
- Account deletion cancels active recurring billing before identity removal and revokes owned grants; disclose loss of access and recovery limitations. Minimal financial records may remain under legal obligations. Do not silently transfer ownership to a partner or use the partner to recover an account.

## Commercial event taxonomy

Future analytics is an opt-in/legally reviewed first-party system, disabled in the scaffold. No cross-site trackers, session replay, fingerprinting, ad pixels or third-party content calls. Server increments bounded daily counters; no general-purpose arbitrary event JSON ingestion.

| Event                                              | Allowed measurement                                                                                                    |
| -------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| landing_view, game_entry                           | Landing-to-game conversion; page from a fixed public route enum, no URL/query/referrer string                          |
| registration_completed                             | Guest-to-account cohort conversion using temporary purpose-limited measurement context only with applicable permission |
| wheel_first_result, recipe_first_plan              | First successful generated result/plan; never record which activity or plan                                            |
| invite_created, invite_accepted                    | Invitations and acceptance totals without relation/member IDs                                                          |
| session_started, return_visit                      | Feature sessions and 7/14/30-day return aggregate; no intimate completion claims                                       |
| pack_preview, checkout_started, purchase_fulfilled | Pack popularity and purchase conversion; public pack SKU only, suppress small sensitive themed cohorts                 |
| refund_completed, subscription_ended               | Financial outcomes aggregated without matching to private play                                                         |
| service_usage_daily                                | Worker/D1/R2/email costs and active-user denominator                                                                   |

Allowed dimensions: day, event enum, broad feature enum, consented acquisition campaign from a small public allowlist, offer experiment arm, currency, public commercial SKU. Never heat, kink/category, answer, activity/result/recipe ID, orientation, identity, relationship ID, private history, raw timestamp stream, full IP, invite token or email. Even pack SKUs can imply interests: keep them out of marketing user profiles, suppress reports below 20 contributors, group sensitive themes, and do not export identifiable purchase histories to ad tools. Financial receipts stay in separate access-controlled commerce records.

Exact retention/return measurement needs linkage. If permitted, derive limited first-party daily/cohort tokens in the application, compute flags/counters and discard links within 30 days; no permanent behavioral profile. Guest “unique visitors” without identifiers is approximate. Publish the measurement limitation rather than covert fingerprinting. D1 aggregate-counter schema is deliberately deferred until this design and local legal consent basis are approved.

## Scorecard and decision rules

Landing conversion = game entries / eligible landing visits. Guest registration = linked registrations / guest starters, reported only when the linkage is legitimately available. First result and first plan count generated **suggestions**, not actual sexual acts. Invitation acceptance = accepted / sent with a 7-day window. Purchase conversion = fulfilled / checkout starts; free-to-paid = new paying accounts / eligible active accounts; avoid a user-versus-couple denominator mismatch. ARPPU = net recognized revenue / paying owners. Retention = return within defined cohort windows, not inferred intimate activity. Cost/MAU includes email and support, not just Workers.

Initial success hypotheses: 20% landing-to-game, 60% entrant-to-first-result, 20% 14-day return, 3% monthly active owners purchasing at $7.99 blended price. These are planning inputs only. Run sequential uncharged concept tests before underwriting, then a small paid pilot; do not charge for unavailable features or advertise a fake discount. At least 30 purchases can expose operational problems but cannot establish a reliable population conversion rate. Report uncertainty and sample size. Pause acquisition spend if refunds exceed the planned 3%, users misunderstand ownership, or support costs exceed contribution. See [Costs](COSTS.md) for conservative high-risk fee and retention sensitivity.
